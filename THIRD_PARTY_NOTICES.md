# Third-party assets

## Voxel Farm

- Creator: The Voxel Store
- Source: https://the-voxel-store.itch.io/voxel-farm
- Files used: goat, sheep and chicken FBX models, their textures, and available animal animation FBX files.
- Usage statement from the creator: assets may be used in games with or without credit.

The original downloaded archive is not committed to the application; only the files required by the simulation are included.
